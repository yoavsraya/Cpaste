What I need from you

Pick one direction, 1, 2 or 3, and any of the four extras. My pick is direction 1 with the mint ring and the fade for long text. It keeps the coloured headers you chose and makes links, colours, code and files recognisable at a glance.

App icons here are stand-in letters. The real app keeps the real icons. Colours are the ones Cpaste already picks from each app's icon.
