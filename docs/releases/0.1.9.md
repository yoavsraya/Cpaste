## New

- **New c↗ branding.** Updated app and menu-bar icons, keeping the familiar clip layout.
- **Smarter clip colours.** Colours are selected from each source app’s icon palette using the same algorithm for every app, with no app-specific overrides. Headers remain fully opaque.
- **More readable labels.** Source colours are toned for readable white text. Category colours stay as chosen, with white or black labels selected for contrast.
- **Compare text clips.** Select two text clips with Cmd+click, then press Space or choose Compare from the right-click menu to see their differences side by side.
- **Preview from the menu.** Right-click a clip and choose Preview to see it in full.
- **Selection and hover feedback.** The selected card glows in its colour, and cards lift slightly under the pointer.

## Install or update

Choose **Check for Updates…** in the Cpaste menu, or download `Cpaste.zip` and replace the app in Applications. Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.
